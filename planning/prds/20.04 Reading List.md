# PRD: Reading List

**Tier:** 2 &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Status:** Proposed &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Owner:** TBD

## Problem
Favorites today are a public endorsement — they show up on `/profile/<username>/favorites` for anyone to see. There's no way for a reader to say "I want to read this later" without publicly broadcasting that they like it. A reading list (private bookmarks, read-later) is a distinct signal from a favorite (public endorsement) and is a baseline expectation in any blogging app worth coming back to.

## Goals
- A logged-in reader can bookmark any article privately.
- Bookmarks live on a new tab on the user's own profile, visible only to them.
- The article card shows a bookmark icon next to the favorite icon — same UX shape, different signal.

## Non-Goals
- Sharing reading lists. They're private. No public bookmark count.
- Folders, tags, or notes on bookmarks. Just a flat list.
- Reading progress (% read). Out of scope; possibly a Tier 3 epic.
- Bookmark notifications to the article author. Bookmarks are private — author doesn't see them.

## User Stories
- As a reader, I click the bookmark icon on an article card; it fills in and the article is added to my reading list.
- As a reader, I visit `/profile/<my-username>/reading-list` and see my bookmarked articles, newest-first.
- As a reader visiting another user's profile, I do **not** see a "Reading List" tab — only my own profile shows it.
- As a reader, I can remove a bookmark from the article card or from the reading list page.
- As a reader, my bookmarks survive even if I unfavorite the article — the two are independent.

## UX Sketch
Bookmark icon (a ribbon or open-book glyph) sits next to the favorite heart on every article card and the article detail page. It does `hx-post` to `/article/<slug>/bookmark`, toggling the M2M and swapping back the icon's filled/unfilled state. On `/profile/<username>` the existing "My Articles | Favorited Articles" tab strip grows a third tab — **but only when `request.user.username == username`**. The reading list tab reuses the article-card partial and the existing pagination helper; the only thing that's new is the queryset and a third route through `_feed_queryset`.

## Data Model Changes
- New M2M on `User`: `bookmarks = models.ManyToManyField("articles.Article", blank=True, related_name="bookmarked_by")`.
- Migration is additive — empty M2M for all existing users.
- No new indexes needed beyond Django's M2M defaults.

## Key Technical Decisions
- **Direct M2M on User**, not a `Bookmark` through-model. We don't need a `created_at` per bookmark for v1. If we ever want "bookmarked on", add a through-model later — it's a one-migration change.
- **Reuse `_feed_queryset` extension point.** The doc note in `apps/articles/CLAUDE.md` explicitly says new feed types (drafts, bookmarks, search) extend `_feed_queryset`. Reading list adds a `feed="bookmarks"` branch.
- **Profile tab visibility is a template guard**, not a view-level redirect. Users hitting `/profile/<other>/reading-list` directly get a 404 from the view — defense in depth.

## Risks & Open Questions
- **Selectors contract.** Bookmark icon is a new selector; nothing currently pinned should break, but the article card layout shifts and that always carries some e2e risk.
- **Tab strip layout.** Three tabs on profile (Articles, Favorites, Reading List) — does that fit the existing nav style? Mock first.
- **Open question:** when an article is deleted, the M2M row drops — the user loses the bookmark with no notification. Acceptable for v1?

## Success Metrics
- Bookmark toggle round-trip <100ms (p95).
- 100% coverage on the new view, M2M, and `_feed_queryset` branch.
- E2e baseline stays green; new e2e covers bookmark + reading-list-tab visibility.

## Rough Effort
S — one M2M, one view, one new feed branch, one tab on the profile template. The pattern is well-trodden in this codebase (favorites is the same shape).

## Dependencies
- None. Touches the same surfaces as favorites; if **20.03 Article Reactions** lands first, the article-card real-estate question gets sharper.
