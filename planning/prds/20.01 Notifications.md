# PRD: Notifications

**Tier:** 2 &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Status:** Proposed &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Owner:** TBD

## Problem
Today an author has no way to know that someone favorited their article, commented on it, or followed them — they'd have to open every article and check the count. For a blogging app where engagement is the whole point, that's a dead loop. Readers also miss the social loop: they favorite an article and never learn the author noticed. A bell icon with a running count of "things that happened to you" closes that gap.

## Goals
- Authors see new favorites, comments, and follows without polling articles by hand.
- Bell icon in the navbar with an unread count, click-to-open dropdown of recent items.
- Notification preferences are at most "on / off per type" — no email digest in this PRD.

## Non-Goals
- Email or push delivery (covered by Tier 1 email infra, if ever).
- Real-time WebSocket updates — HTMX polling is enough.
- Notifications for tags, drafts, or mentions (`@user` doesn't exist yet).

## User Stories
- As an author, I see a "3" badge on the bell when 3 new things happened since I last looked.
- As an author, I open the dropdown and see "janedoe favorited *How to Train Your Dragon*" with a link to the article.
- As any user, I click a notification and it's marked read; opening the dropdown marks all visible items read.
- As any user, I can mute "follows" notifications from settings while keeping favorites and comments.

## UX Sketch
New `bell` partial slotted into `templates/_navbar.html`, visible only when authenticated. The bell polls `GET /notifications/unread-count` every 30s via `hx-trigger="every 30s"` and swaps a small badge. Clicking the bell does `hx-get="/notifications/dropdown"` and swaps in a panel with the 10 most recent items. A "see all" link goes to `/notifications/`, a full-page list with pagination. Each row links to the source object (article, profile). Mark-read happens server-side when the dropdown is fetched.

## Data Model Changes
- New app `apps/notifications/` with model `Notification(recipient, actor, verb, target_type, target_id, created, read_at)`.
- `verb` is a small enum: `favorited`, `commented`, `followed`. `target_*` is a generic FK (Django `contenttypes`) to `Article` or `User`.
- Index on `(recipient, read_at, created)` for the unread-count and recent queries.
- New `User.notification_prefs` JSONField, default `{"favorites": true, "comments": true, "follows": true}`.

## Key Technical Decisions
- **Roll our own** rather than `django-notifications-hq`. The library is unmaintained (last release 2023, Django 4.x pinned), and our model is small enough that 50 lines of code beats an abandoned dependency.
- **Synchronous fan-out** in the view that creates the source event (favorite, comment, follow). One INSERT per follower-of-author is fine at our scale; revisit when an article has 1k+ favorites/min.
- **Polling, not SSE.** HTMX `every 30s` is one line of template; SSE/Channels adds an ASGI server and a whole new infra story for a low-value win.

## Risks & Open Questions
- **Self-notifications.** Don't notify users about their own actions — easy to forget on the comment path. Add a guard in the creator helper.
- **Cascade on delete.** If an article is deleted, do its notifications go? Yes — cascade via the generic FK requires a signal handler, since contenttypes doesn't auto-cascade.
- **Open question:** do we collapse "5 people favorited X" into one row, or show 5 rows? Start with 5 rows; revisit if the dropdown gets noisy.

## Success Metrics
- Bell partial renders in <50ms (p95) — it runs on every authenticated page.
- Zero regressions in the e2e baseline (74 passed).
- New unit tests bring the app to 100% coverage at the gate.

## Rough Effort
M — one new app, one new model, one navbar partial, fan-out hooks in three existing views. No migration risk to existing data.

## Dependencies
- None hard. Pairs naturally with **20.05 Rich Profiles** (follower count visibility) and **10.01** if email digest is added later.
