# PRD: Moderation

**Tier:** 3 &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Status:** Proposed &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Owner:** TBD

## Problem
There is no way for a reader to flag an abusive or spammy article or comment, no queue for staff to triage reports, and no rate limit on signup or comment posting. `comment_create_view` and `register_view` accept unlimited POSTs from any IP. Article and comment deletes are hard deletes — once gone, the audit trail is gone with them. As soon as the site is publicly indexed, the first spam wave will overrun us, and we have no tools to respond.

## Goals
- Any logged-in user can report any article or comment with one click.
- Staff have a queue (admin or custom) showing open reports, with actions: dismiss, soft-delete, ban author.
- Soft-deletion replaces hard-delete for articles and comments — content disappears from the public site but is recoverable for 30 days.
- Signup and comment endpoints are rate-limited per-IP and per-user.

## Non-Goals
- Automated content moderation (Perspective API, OpenAI moderation, etc.). We collect signal first; automation is a follow-up if volume warrants.
- Public moderation logs / community moderators. Staff-only for v1.
- Appeal flows. A banned user emails support; no in-app appeal UI yet.

## User Stories
- As a reader, I can click "Report" on an article or comment, pick a reason (spam, harassment, illegal, other), and submit.
- As a staff member, I can open `/admin/moderation/` and see all open reports, sorted by recency, with a one-click "soft-delete" action.
- As a staff member, I can soft-delete a comment and the comment disappears from the article page within one HTMX swap.
- As a spammer, I cannot register more than 5 accounts per hour or post more than 10 comments per minute from one IP.
- As a victim of a mistaken takedown, an admin can restore my soft-deleted article within 30 days.

## UX Sketch
**Report button:** a small `Report` link on every article (article detail meta line) and every comment (in the comment card). Clicking opens a tiny HTMX-loaded modal with a `<select>` for reason and a textarea for context, posting to `report_create_view` and replacing the link with `Reported`.

**Admin queue:** a custom `ModelAdmin` in `apps/moderation/admin.py` with list filters (status, content type, reason), bulk actions ("dismiss selected", "soft-delete target content"), and a detail page that links to the reported object.

**Soft-delete:** a `deleted_at` timestamp on `Article` and `Comment`. Default managers (`objects`) filter `deleted_at__isnull=True`; an `all_objects` manager exposes everything for admin and recovery.

## Data Model Changes
- New app `apps/moderation/`. Adds dependency directions: `moderation → articles`, `moderation → comments`, `moderation → accounts`. Stays a leaf (nothing imports from it).
- New `Report` model: `reporter (FK User), content_type (FK ContentType), object_id (PositiveIntegerField), reason (CharField with choices), context (TextField, blank), status (CharField: open|dismissed|actioned), created, resolved_at (nullable), resolver (FK User, nullable)`. Generic FK keeps it usable for both articles and comments without two parallel tables.
- `Article.deleted_at: DateTimeField(null=True, db_index=True)` and `Comment.deleted_at` — additive, nullable. Default manager updated to filter; `all_objects = models.Manager()` retains unfiltered access for admin.
- Index on `Report(status, created)` for fast queue rendering.

## Key Technical Decisions
- **`django-ratelimit`** for rate limiting (decorator on `register_view`, `comment_create_view`, and `report_create_view` itself to prevent report-spam). Mature, no Redis dependency required for in-memory cache backend; alternative considered was rolling our own with the cache framework — `django-ratelimit` already does it correctly.
- **Custom `ModelAdmin` over a third-party moderation package.** Django's admin is already there, and our scale doesn't justify pulling in something like `django-flagging`. Revisit when staff is more than two people.
- **Soft-delete via column, not via separate "trash" table.** Column is one migration and one manager change; trash table is two more queries on every list page. Tradeoff: soft-deleted rows stay in the same table — fine at our scale, would need partitioning at very large scale.
- Rate limits start permissive (signup: 5/hour/IP, comment: 10/min/user, report: 20/hour/user) and tighten after we see real traffic.
- Generic FK on `Report` over two `nullable=True` FKs (`article` + `comment`) — generic FK is uglier in queries but trivially extends to future content types (e.g. user profiles).

## Risks & Open Questions
- **Risk:** Coordinated false-reports as a takedown weapon. Mitigation: status stays `open` until a human reviews; `actioned` is the only state that hides content. Reports themselves do nothing automatically.
- **Risk:** Soft-delete forgotten about, table grows forever. Mitigation: management command to hard-delete soft-deleted rows older than 30 days; runs nightly.
- **Open question:** Notify the reporter when their report is actioned/dismissed? Probably yes — closes the loop. Defer the email piece if it adds scope.
- **Open question:** Show "this content was removed by moderators" tombstones, or just disappear it? Recommend a tombstone on articles (preserves URL), nothing for comments.

## Success Metrics
- Time-to-action on a report < 24h (staff SLO).
- 0 hard-deleted articles in production after launch (operational invariant).
- Spam-comment volume reaching the public site drops measurably after rate limits enable.

## Rough Effort
L — new app, new model with generic FK, manager-level filtering on two existing models, admin customization, three rate-limit decorators, e2e coverage for the report flow.

## Dependencies
- **10.01 Password Reset and Email Verification** (Tier 1) — verified email is the precondition that makes per-user rate limits meaningful. Without it, attackers churn through unverified accounts.
- **20.05 Notifications** (Tier 2, if it lands) — would carry the "your report was actioned" message; otherwise email it directly.
