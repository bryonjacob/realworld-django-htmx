# PRD: Article Reactions

**Tier:** 2 &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Status:** Proposed &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Owner:** TBD

## Problem
Today, `Article.favorites` is a binary M2M: a user has favorited or hasn't. That's a blunt signal — there's no way to express "I liked this a lot" vs. "I liked this." Authors want a richer engagement signal; readers want to express enthusiasm without the all-or-nothing of a favorite. Medium's claps (1–50 per user) and Slack-style emoji reactions are the two well-known shapes; we should pick one and ship it.

## Goals
- Readers can react to articles with more granularity than a single favorite.
- The article card shows an aggregated reaction count or breakdown.
- Existing favorite behavior (the public "endorsed" signal) is untouched.

## Non-Goals
- Reactions on comments. Articles only.
- Replacing favorites. They're a different signal — public endorsement vs. casual reaction.
- Custom emoji uploads. Fixed reaction set.

## User Stories
- As a reader, I tap a clap button on an article; the count goes up by 1 (or by however many I clap, up to 50).
- As a reader, I see the total clap count on every article card and the article detail page.
- As a logged-out reader, the clap button is disabled with a tooltip pointing to login.
- As an author, I see a breakdown on my profile or article: "324 claps from 87 readers."
- As a reader, I un-clap (set my count back to 0) by clicking and dragging the count down — or just clicking again to decrement.

## UX Sketch
**Decision: Medium-style claps (1–50 per user).** The favorite button on the article card stays as the public endorsement signal. A new clap button sits next to it, showing a hand icon and the aggregate count. Clicking does `hx-post` to `/article/<slug>/clap`, which increments the user's clap count by 1 (capped at 50) and swaps back the updated count. On the article detail page, a more elaborate widget shows your contribution and the total. No new page or nav surface — this is purely a partial swap on existing surfaces.

## Data Model Changes
- New model `ArticleReaction(user, article, count, created, updated)` with unique constraint on `(user, article)`.
- `count` is `PositiveSmallIntegerField` capped at 50 in form/view validation.
- New `ArticleQuerySet.with_claps()` annotation: `total_claps = Sum("articlereaction__count")`. Lives next to existing `with_favorites`.
- Migration is additive; no backfill.

## Key Technical Decisions
- **Claps over emoji reactions.** Emoji reactions need a picker UI, an icon set, and a "what does :rocket: even mean here" UX problem. Claps are one button, one number, one mental model. Alternative considered: 5 fixed emoji (`heart`, `clap`, `thinking`, `fire`, `eyes`) — rejected as visually cluttered for an article card.
- **One row per (user, article)**, mutating `count` — not one row per clap. Avoids a high-write table and trivializes the aggregation query.
- **Optimistic HTMX swap.** The button does `hx-post` and replaces only the count span. No full re-render of the article card.

## Risks & Open Questions
- **Article card real estate.** Three engagement signals on a card (favorite ♥, clap 👏, comment count) is busy. Mock and review before committing.
- **Selectors contract.** `realworld/specs/e2e/SELECTORS.md` pins favorite-button selectors. The new clap button must not collide.
- **Open question:** should claps factor into the popular feed sort? Probably not in v1 — keep "Global Feed" as recency-sorted; claps are a separate display-only signal until we have an explicit "popular" tab.

## Success Metrics
- Clap action round-trip <100ms (p95).
- New `ArticleReaction` model at 100% coverage.
- E2e baseline stays green; one new e2e for the clap path.

## Rough Effort
S — one model, one view, one template partial. No migration risk.

## Dependencies
- None. Composable with **20.05 Rich Profiles** (show top-clapped articles on profile).
